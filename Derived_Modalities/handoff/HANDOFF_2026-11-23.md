## 1. Project in one paragraph

Project C asks whether functional modalities derived from gene expression can act as extra layers in multimodal SpatialLeiden. Examples are pathway activity (PROGENy), transcription-factor activity (CollecTRI) and cell-cell signalling (LIANA+).

The plan:

1. Choose modalities and tools for the Kuppe et al. 2022 myocardial infarction Visium data.
1. Derive the modalities.
1. Cluster each one on its own with SpatialLeiden and compare.
1. Combine them across a grid of layer weights in `spatialleiden_multimodal`.

The step-by-step plan and folder layout are in `README.md`.

**Things to know before using the Visium data** (details in `01_data_preprocess/kuppe_visium_exploration/NOTES.md`):

1. **Use the authors' Seurat objects, not the Zenodo h5ad.** The Seurat objects sit inside each Space Ranger archive (`10X_Visium_<id>.tar.gz` → `<id>/<id>.rds`).
   - They contain the published `celltype_niche` and `molecular_niche` labels, cell2location abundances (`c2l`) and proportions (`c2l_props`), and the real spot coordinates.
   - The h5ad files have none of the niches, and their coordinates are rescaled to 0–1 separately along x and y.
1. **There are 28 Visium runs from 27 tissue samples, with 91,517 spots in total** (the paper's number). Remote zone of patient P6 (RZ_P6) was run twice (ACH0021 and ACH0022).
1. **Zones are per section, not per spot.** `major_labl` collapses mixed regions, e.g. RZ/BZ counts as BZ and IZ/BZ as IZ.
1. **Sample IDs come in three forms.** `metadata-Visium.csv` links them:
   - region: `GT/IZ_P9`,
   - run: `ACH0012`,
   - H&E file: `Visium_9_CK287`.
1. **Use `scalefactors_json.json`, not the Seurat object, for the spot diameter.** The value stored in the Seurat objects is wrong.
1. **Mitochondrial fraction is 31–40 % per spot in healthy myocardium,** so a standard 20 % filter would remove most of it.

## 5. Visium exploration (done)

A descriptive-only pass, following the exploration brief and limited to the Visium sections. It uses only what ships with the data: no new modalities, clustering, deconvolution or integration.

**Code:** `01_data_preprocess/kuppe_visium_exploration/`. Run `run_all.sh` from `Derived_Modalities/`. It needs two conda environments: one with R and Seurat, and one with Python, scanpy and openpyxl. It runs:

| Step | Script | Does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `01_extract_seurat_tables.R` | Writes a per-spot table from each Seurat object (niches, cell2location, coordinates, QC) |
| 2 | `02_extract_spaceranger_files.py` | Copies the low-resolution H&E, scale factors and Space Ranger metrics |
| 3 | `03_figures.py` | Makes all figures, plus `stats.json` and summary tables |
| 4 | `04_inventory.py` | Writes `data_inventory.csv`, one row per downloaded file |
| 5 | `05_report_tables.py` + `report/kuppe_visium_exploration.tex` | Builds the PDF report (12 pages) |

**Outputs:** `results/kuppe_2022/visium_exploration/`, containing the PDF, figures, CSV tables and `NOTES.md`. Copies of `NOTES.md` and `data_inventory.csv` are kept in git.

**Findings:**

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Matched snRNA + snATAC + Visium? | All 27 tissue samples, according to the authors' sample tables. snATAC is thin in ischaemic samples (a median of 434 nuclei). These counts are not yet checked against the objects. |
| Border-zone slides? | Three, all mixed remote/border (P2, P3, P12), all usable. Median UMIs per spot: 4,904, 7,400 and 2,829. |
| Depth by zone? | Fibrotic sections are the shallowest (1,797 UMIs and 1.3 estimated cells per spot, against about 3,000–5,000 UMIs elsewhere). Estimated cells per spot track depth (ρ = 0.84), so they are no independent cellularity measure. Nuclei counts from H&E were not deposited. |
| Niche coherence? | The share of neighbouring spots with the same label is 0.39 for cell-type niches (0.25 if shuffled) and 0.47 for molecular niches (0.33). Cell-type niches are coherent in ischaemic and fibrotic tissue and close to chance in myogenic tissue. This is the baseline SpatialLeiden must beat. |
| H&E for every slide? | Yes, all 28. Only the two RZ_P6 images cannot be matched to their runs. |
| Donor effect in the embeddings? | Not covered: it needs the snRNA-seq data, which is outside the Visium scope. |

## 6. Decisions so far

- **Start with the Visium sections.** The single-nucleus data comes later.
- **Keep the first pass descriptive.** New computation starts with the Figure 2 recreation.
- **Possible snATAC-seq uses** (not decided):
  - (A) Map transcription-factor activity per cell type from snATAC onto spots, weighted by the spots' cell-type proportions. The paper used this approach (Methods, "Mapping transcription factor binding activity…").
  - (B) Cluster RNA and ATAC together without space. This needs the two assays paired, and they are separate nuclei.
- **Shipped cell-type proportions are a ready-made modality.** cell2location proportions come with the data, so they can join the candidate list in `00_strategy/`.
